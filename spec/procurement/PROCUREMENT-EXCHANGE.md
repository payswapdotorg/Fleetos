# Procurement Exchange Model

Fleet is the orchestrator; local vendors are independent fulfillment providers.

Demand:
DRAFT -> SUBMITTED -> MATCHING -> QUOTED -> ACCEPTED -> FULFILLING -> DELIVERED -> VERIFIED -> CLOSED

Matching considers workload requirement, quantity, deadline, delivery area, substitutions, budget, warranty, SLA, vendor quality and inventory.

Compatible orders may be grouped. Grouping preserves per-customer contract identity and auditable price allocation.

Vendor outcomes include final price, delivery time, failure rate, warranty outcome, return rate, customer satisfaction and maintenance performance.
